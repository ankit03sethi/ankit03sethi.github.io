FAQs

Quick answers

Which GST returns are covered?

Every plan covers monthly GSTR-1 (outward supplies) and GSTR-3B (summary + tax payment). GSTR-9 (annual return) is included in the Yearly plan; add-on for Monthly/Quarterly.

When do you file each month?

We reconcile between the 1st and 10th, and file GSTR-1 by the 11th and GSTR-3B by the 20th (as per GST rules) — always well before the government deadline.

Are government fees / GST payable included?

Our service fee is all-inclusive. The GST amount payable to the government (based on your sales) is separate — we tell you exactly how much to pay before we file.

What if I miss sharing data one month?

We send WhatsApp reminders 3 days before the due date. If we don't hear back, we file a NIL return to avoid penalty. You can revise later.

Can I cancel and get a refund?

Monthly plans are per-month — no refund for the month already filed. Yearly and Quarterly plans get a pro-rata refund for the unfiled months.

Do I need existing GST to buy this plan?

Yes — you must already have a GSTIN. If you don't, first register with our GST Registration service (₹999) and then subscribe to filing.
