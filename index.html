About Cursive Private Limited

We are a New Delhi based company that sells balloons and party decoration products online under the cursive® brand (a registered trademark) and the Shopperskart storefront. Every order is packed at our own warehouse in Hari Nagar and handed to marketplace courier partners the same day.

Our in-house team manages listings, stock, pricing and settlements for our own marketplace accounts using software we built for ourselves, so customers get accurate stock and fast dispatch.
