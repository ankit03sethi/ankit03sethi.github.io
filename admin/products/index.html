🖼 Bulk upload images

Pick a folder — or select the files — named SKU-position. Do not type the extension. s175-1 is the main image for SKU S175, s175-2 its second, up to 7. s175-video becomes the video.

Upload the product sheet first — a filename only matches a SKU that already exists. Images on a matched SKU are replaced; every other product is left alone.
